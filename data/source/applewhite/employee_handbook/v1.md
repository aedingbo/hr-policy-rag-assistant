## 1. Welcome

Applewhite Health operates outpatient clinics in Georgia, South Carolina and North Carolina,
with its headquarters in Georgia. This handbook gives an overview of how we work. Where a topic
has its own policy, that policy is the source of truth.

## 2. Who this handbook applies to

All Applewhite Health employees in Georgia (GA), South Carolina (SC) and North Carolina (NC).
Employees fall into two groups:

- **Clinical staff:** physicians, nurse practitioners, nurses, medical assistants and
  technicians who provide patient care.
- **Non-clinical staff:** front desk, billing, scheduling, IT and administration.

## 3. Patient privacy

- Access patient records only when needed for your job.
- Never discuss patient information in public areas or on personal devices.
- Report a suspected privacy incident to your manager the same day.

## 4. Work hours and attendance

- Clinic hours vary by location. Schedules are published two weeks in advance.
- If you will be absent, call your clinic manager at least two hours before your shift so
  patient appointments can be covered.
- Shift swaps must be approved by the clinic manager.

## 5. Paid time off at a glance

Full PTO rules are in the PTO and Leave Policy. In summary:

- PTO is requested through the scheduling system and approved by your clinic manager.
- Employees may carry over **up to 40 hours** of unused PTO into the next calendar year.

## 6. Conduct

- Treat patients, families and coworkers with respect and professionalism.
- Wear your Applewhite identification badge at all times in clinics.
- Harassment, discrimination and retaliation are not tolerated.
