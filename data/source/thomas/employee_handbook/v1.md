## 1. Welcome

Thomas Software is a remote-first software company headquartered in Colorado, with employees in
Colorado, Texas, Washington, New York, Illinois and Arizona. Most of us work from home; our
Denver office is available for anyone who prefers it. This handbook explains how we work
together. Where a topic has its own policy, that policy is the source of truth.

## 2. How we work

- **Core hours:** 11:00–15:00 Eastern Time on weekdays, so teams across time zones overlap.
  Outside core hours, schedules are flexible.
- **Written first:** decisions are written down in the team's shared documents so people in other
  time zones can follow them.
- **Meetings:** keep them short, share an agenda in advance, and record decisions.

## 3. Security

- Use only your company laptop for company work. Keep it encrypted and updated.
- Turn on multi-factor authentication for every company account.
- Report a lost device or suspicious email to the Security team immediately.

## 4. Conduct

- Be respectful in every channel — chat, video and email.
- Harassment, discrimination and retaliation are not tolerated.
- Raise concerns with your manager, any leader or People Operations.

## 6. Pay

Employees are paid twice a month, on the 15th and the last business day of the month.
