## 1. Welcome

Grayer Logistics provides freight transportation and warehousing services from its
headquarters in Ohio and facilities in Indiana, Kentucky and Pennsylvania. This handbook
gives an overview of how we work. Where a topic has its own policy, the handbook points to it,
and **the specific policy is the source of truth**.

## 2. Who this handbook applies to

This handbook applies to all Grayer Logistics employees in all four operating states:
Ohio (OH), Indiana (IN), Kentucky (KY) and Pennsylvania (PA). Employees fall into three
groups:

- **Operations employees:** warehouse associates, equipment operators and drivers.
- **Office employees:** dispatch, finance, sales, IT and administration.
- **People managers:** anyone who directly supervises other employees.

## 3. Work hours and attendance

- Standard full-time schedules are 40 hours per week. Part-time schedules are under 30 hours.
- Operations shifts are posted at least one week in advance by the site supervisor.
- If you will be absent or late, notify your supervisor before the start of your shift.
- Three unreported absences in a rolling 90-day period may lead to corrective action.

## 4. Conduct and safety

- Treat coworkers, drivers and customers with respect. Harassment and discrimination are
  not tolerated.
- Follow all posted safety rules. Personal protective equipment is required in warehouse and
  yard areas.
- Report injuries, near misses and unsafe conditions to your supervisor the same day.
- Operating powered equipment requires a current Grayer equipment certification.

## 6. Pay

- Employees are paid every two weeks, on Fridays.
- Overtime for eligible employees is paid at 1.5 times the regular rate for hours over 40 in
  a work week and must be approved in advance by a supervisor.
- Report any pay error to People Operations within 30 days of the pay date.

## 7. Raising concerns

You can raise a concern with your supervisor, any manager, or People Operations. Grayer does
not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## 8. Changes to this handbook

People Operations reviews this handbook at least once a year. When a policy changes, the new
version and its effective date are published on the HR portal. The version in effect on a
given date applies to events on that date.
