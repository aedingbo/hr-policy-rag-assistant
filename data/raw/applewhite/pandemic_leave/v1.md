## 1. Purpose

This temporary policy provides paid leave during a declared public health emergency so that
employees who are ill or exposed do not come to work in our clinics.

## 2. Pandemic leave

- All employees may take up to **10 days of paid pandemic leave** if they:
  - test positive for the pandemic illness,
  - are told to isolate or quarantine by a healthcare provider or public health authority, or
  - must care for a household member who is isolating.
- Pandemic leave is separate from PTO and does not reduce your PTO balance.
- Pandemic leave is paid at your regular base rate.

## 3. How to request

1. Notify your clinic manager before your shift.
2. Submit the pandemic leave form on the HR portal within 3 days.
3. Provide a test result or provider note when available.

## 4. Returning to work

Clinical staff must complete the return-to-work screening with Employee Health before seeing
patients.

## 5. Duration

This policy applies while a public health emergency is declared and may be withdrawn by People
Operations at any time.
