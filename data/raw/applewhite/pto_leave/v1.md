## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how paid time off (PTO) is earned and used.

## 2. PTO accrual

- Full-time employees accrue **18 days of PTO per year** (144 hours), earned each pay period.
- Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their scheduled hours.
- PTO can be used after 60 days of employment.

## 3. Carryover

- You may carry over **up to 40 hours** of unused PTO into the next calendar year.
- Unused PTO above 40 hours is forfeited on December 31.

## 4. Requesting PTO

- Request PTO in the scheduling system at least **four weeks** before the schedule period, so
  patient appointments can be planned.
- Your clinic manager approves requests based on clinic coverage.
- For unplanned absences, call your clinic manager at least two hours before your shift.

## 5. Holidays

Clinics close on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and
Christmas Day. These are paid holidays for scheduled employees.

## 6. Other leave

| Leave type | Length | Paid? |
|---|---|---|
| Bereavement | Up to 3 days | Yes |
| Jury duty | As required | Yes |
| Medical or family leave | Up to 12 weeks for eligible employees | Unpaid; PTO may be used |

Contact People Operations to request bereavement, jury, medical or family leave.
