## 1. Purpose

This guide explains what happens during your first weeks at Applewhite Health.

## 2. Before your first day

- Complete the new-hire forms sent by Talent Acquisition.
- Clinical staff: upload copies of your professional license, certifications and immunization
  records.

## 3. Credential verification (clinical staff)

**Clinical staff may not have any patient contact until credential verification is complete.**
Talent Acquisition verifies:

| Item | Verified with |
|---|---|
| Professional license | State licensing board for the clinic's state |
| Board certification (where required) | Certifying board |
| Basic life support certification | Issuing organization |
| Immunizations | Employee Health |

Verification usually takes 3–5 business days. Until it is complete, clinical staff attend
orientation and non-patient training only.

## 4. First two weeks

| Week | Activity |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | Orientation, patient privacy training, systems access, clinic tour |
| Week 2 | Shadowing in your clinic; job-specific training |

## 5. First 90 days

Your manager meets with you at 30 and 90 days. Benefits start on the first of the month after
your hire date (see the Benefits Overview and Enrollment).

## 6. Questions

Contact Talent Acquisition through the HR portal.
