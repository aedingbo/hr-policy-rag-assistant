## 1. Purpose

This policy explains which business expenses Applewhite reimburses and how to claim them.

## 2. General rules

- Expenses must be necessary for Applewhite business and approved by your manager.
- Submit claims **within 60 days** of the expense date. Later claims are not reimbursed.
- **Receipts are required for any expense over $50.**

## 3. What is reimbursed

| Expense | Rule |
|---|---|
| Travel between clinics during a shift | Mileage at the current IRS standard rate |
| Conference travel | Pre-approved by your manager; economy airfare and standard hotel |
| Meals while traveling overnight | Up to $55 per day |
| Professional license renewal | Reimbursed for the license required by your role |

## 4. What is not reimbursed

- Commuting between home and your assigned clinic.
- Parking or traffic fines.
- Personal items, alcohol and entertainment.

## 5. How to submit

1. Submit the claim and receipts in the expense system.
2. Your manager approves the claim.
3. Finance pays approved claims with the next regular payroll.

## 6. Questions

Contact Finance through the expense system.
