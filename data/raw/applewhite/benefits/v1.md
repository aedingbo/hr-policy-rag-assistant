## 1. Purpose

This document explains the benefits Applewhite Health offers, who is eligible and how to enroll.

## 2. Eligibility

- Employees regularly scheduled for **24 or more hours per week** are eligible for medical,
  dental and vision coverage.
- Coverage starts on the **first day of the month after your hire date**. Example: if you are
  hired on March 12, coverage starts April 1. If you are hired on the first day of a month,
  coverage starts on the first day of the following month.
- Per-diem staff are not eligible for medical, dental or vision coverage.

## 3. Plans offered

| Benefit | Options | Employee cost |
|---|---|---|
| Medical | PPO or HMO | Shared; rates on the HR portal |
| Dental | Basic or Enhanced | Shared |
| Vision | One plan | Employee-paid |
| Life insurance | 2× annual salary | Company-paid |
| Long-term disability | 60% of base pay after 90 days | Company-paid |

## 4. Retirement plan

Eligible employees may join the 403(b) plan after 60 days of employment. Applewhite matches 100%
of contributions up to 4% of eligible pay.

## 5. Continuing education

Clinical staff receive up to $1,000 per calendar year for required continuing education.
Submit requests through Benefits Administration.

## 6. How to enroll

1. Benefits Administration sends an enrollment notice in your first week.
2. Make your elections on the HR portal **before your coverage start date**.
3. If you miss the deadline, you can enroll at annual open enrollment (each October, for coverage
   starting January 1) or after a qualifying life event.

## 7. Questions

Contact Benefits Administration through the HR portal. Plan documents govern if they differ from
this overview.
