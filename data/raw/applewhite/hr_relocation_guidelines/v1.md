## 1. Purpose

These guidelines tell People Operations how to handle pay and licensing when an approved
relocation changes an employee's state.

## 2. Pay adjustment by state

Base pay is adjusted by the state factor. The change applies from the first full pay period
after the move.

| State | Factor |
|---|---|
| Georgia (GA) | 1.00 |
| North Carolina (NC) | 1.02 |
| South Carolina (SC) | 0.97 |

**Calculation:** new base pay = current base pay × (new state factor ÷ current state factor).
Example: a billing specialist earning $50,000 who moves from Georgia to South Carolina has a new
base pay of $50,000 × (0.97 ÷ 1.00) = $48,500.

## 3. Clinical licensure transfer

- A clinician may not see patients in the new state until they hold an **active license in that
  state**. HR Compliance verifies the license before the first scheduled shift.
- Applewhite reimburses license application fees **up to $750** for company-approved clinic
  transfers.
- If the license is not issued by the planned start date, the employee may be placed on
  non-patient duties or unpaid leave for up to 60 days.

## 4. Relocation assistance

Applewhite does not provide relocation assistance for employee-requested moves. For
company-requested clinic transfers, HR may approve up to **$3,000** in moving expenses with
receipts.

## 5. Records

Store license verification, approvals and pay calculations in the employee's HR file. Do not
share state factors with managers or employees.
