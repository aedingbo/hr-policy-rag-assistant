## 1. Purpose

This policy explains who may work remotely and what is required before moving to a new state.

## 2. Clinical roles

**Clinical roles are on-site only.** Physicians, nurse practitioners, nurses, medical assistants
and technicians provide in-person patient care and are not eligible for remote work.

## 3. Non-clinical roles

- Non-clinical staff (billing, scheduling, IT, administration) may work remotely, fully or part
  of the week, with **People Operations (HR) approval**.
- Remote work is allowed only from Applewhite's operating states: **Georgia, South Carolina
  and North Carolina**.

## 4. Moving to another state

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Non-clinical employee moving within GA, SC or NC | Submit a relocation request on the HR portal at least **45 days** before the move; People Operations approves |
| Non-clinical employee moving outside GA, SC or NC | Cannot be approved for remote work in the current role; contact People Operations |
| Clinical employee moving to another state | Must transfer to a clinic in that state, if one exists, and hold an active license there before seeing patients; contact People Operations |

Your manager is informed of the decision but does not approve relocations.

## 5. Equipment

Applewhite provides a laptop and headset for approved remote work. Patient information must only
be accessed on company devices.

## 6. Questions

Contact People Operations through the HR portal.
