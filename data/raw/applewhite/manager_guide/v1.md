## 1. Purpose

This guide helps clinic managers approve schedules, shift swaps and time off while keeping
every clinic safely staffed.

## 2. Minimum clinical coverage

| Clinic size | Minimum on every open shift |
|---|---|
| Small (1–3 exam rooms) | 1 licensed provider + 1 nurse or medical assistant |
| Medium (4–8 exam rooms) | 2 licensed providers + 2 nurses or medical assistants |
| Large (9+ exam rooms) | 3 licensed providers + 3 nurses or medical assistants |

Never approve time off or a shift swap that drops a shift below the minimum.

## 3. Approving PTO

1. Check the request against the PTO and Leave Policy **version in effect** for the requested
   dates (request timing, balances and carryover rules are in that policy).
2. Check minimum coverage for every affected shift.
3. If coverage would fall below the minimum, offer the employee alternative dates or seek
   coverage from the float pool before declining.
4. Respond within 5 business days.

## 4. Shift swaps

Approve swaps only between employees with the same role and current credentials. Record the swap
in the scheduling system.

## 5. Relocation requests

Clinic managers do not approve relocations. Direct employees to People Operations and the Remote
Work and Relocation Policy.

## 6. Escalation

Contact People Operations about repeated coverage gaps, disputes or any request that may involve
medical or family leave.
