## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how paid time off (PTO) is earned and used, and what other leave is
available.

## 2. PTO accrual

- Full-time employees accrue **15 days of PTO per year**, earned at 1.25 days per month of
  service.
- Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their scheduled hours.
- PTO accrual starts on the first day of employment. PTO can be used after 90 days of
  employment.

## 3. Carryover

- You may carry over **up to 5 unused PTO days** into the next calendar year.
- Unused PTO above 5 days is forfeited on December 31.
- PTO is not paid out at the end of the year.

## 4. Requesting PTO

| Length of absence | Request at least |
|---|---|
| 1–2 days | 3 days in advance |
| 3 or more consecutive days | 2 weeks in advance |

- Submit requests on the HR portal. Your manager approves or declines them.
- Operations sites may limit the number of people off on the same shift.
- If you are sick, you may use PTO without advance notice. Notify your supervisor before your
  shift starts.

## 5. Holidays

Grayer observes six paid holidays: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day,
Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. Operations employees scheduled to work on a holiday receive
holiday pay at 1.5 times their regular rate.

## 6. Other leave

| Leave type | Length | Paid? |
|---|---|---|
| Bereavement (immediate family) | Up to 3 days | Yes |
| Jury duty | As required | Yes, up to 10 days |
| Military leave | As required by law | Per applicable law |
| Medical or family leave | Up to 12 weeks for eligible employees | Unpaid; PTO may be used |

Contact People Operations to request bereavement, jury, military, medical or family leave.

## 7. Leaving Grayer

Accrued, unused PTO is paid out at the end of employment if you give at least two weeks'
notice.
