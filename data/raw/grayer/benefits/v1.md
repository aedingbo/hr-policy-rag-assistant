## 1. Purpose

This document explains which benefits Grayer Logistics offers, who is eligible, and how to
enroll.

## 2. Eligibility

- **Medical, dental and vision coverage:** employees who are regularly scheduled to work
  **30 or more hours per week** become eligible after completing **30 days of employment**.
  Coverage starts on day 31.
- Employees scheduled for fewer than 30 hours per week are not eligible for medical, dental or
  vision coverage, but may join the retirement plan (section 4).
- If your scheduled hours change to 30 or more per week, the 30-day waiting period starts on
  the date of the change.

## 3. Plans offered

| Benefit | Options | Employee cost |
|---|---|---|
| Medical | Standard plan or High-deductible plan with HSA | Shared; rates on the HR portal |
| Dental | One plan | Shared |
| Vision | One plan | Employee-paid |
| Basic life insurance | 1× annual salary | Company-paid |
| Short-term disability | 60% of base pay, up to 12 weeks | Company-paid |

## 4. Retirement plan

All employees aged 18 or older may join the 401(k) plan from their first paycheck. Grayer
matches 50% of employee contributions up to 6% of eligible pay.

## 5. How to enroll

1. You receive an enrollment notice from Benefits Administration when you become eligible.
2. Make your elections on the HR portal within **31 days** of becoming eligible.
3. If you do not enroll within 31 days, you can next enroll during annual open enrollment
   (held each November for coverage starting January 1), unless you have a qualifying life
   event.

## 6. Qualifying life events

You may change your elections within 31 days of a qualifying life event, such as marriage,
birth or adoption of a child, divorce, or loss of other coverage. Submit proof of the event on
the HR portal.

## 7. Questions

Contact Benefits Administration through the HR portal. Benefit plan documents govern if they
differ from this overview.
