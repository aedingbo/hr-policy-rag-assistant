## 1. Purpose

This guide explains what happens during your first weeks at Grayer Logistics.

## 2. Before your first day

- Complete the new-hire forms sent by Talent Acquisition, including tax and direct-deposit forms.
- Bring identification documents for employment eligibility verification on your first day.

## 3. Week one

| Day | Activity |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Welcome session, HR portal setup, badge and equipment issue |
| Days 1–3 | **Safety training (required)**: site safety rules, protective equipment, emergency procedures and injury reporting |
| Days 3–5 | Job-specific training with your supervisor or trainer |

**Safety training must be completed in week one**, before you work in warehouse or yard areas.
Office employees complete the same safety module because they may visit operations sites.

## 4. Equipment certification

Operations employees who will use powered equipment (for example forklifts) must pass the
Grayer equipment certification before operating it. Certification is scheduled during the
first two weeks.

## 5. First 90 days

- Your supervisor meets with you at 30, 60 and 90 days to review progress.
- PTO accrues from day one and can be used after 90 days (see the PTO and Leave Policy).
- You become eligible for medical coverage after 30 days if you work 30 or more hours per week
  (see the Benefits Overview and Enrollment).

## 6. Questions

Contact Talent Acquisition for paperwork questions and your supervisor for training questions.
