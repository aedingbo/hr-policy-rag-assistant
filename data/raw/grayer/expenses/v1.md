## 1. Purpose

This policy explains which business expenses Grayer reimburses and how to claim them.

## 2. General rules

- Expenses must be reasonable, necessary for Grayer business, and approved by your manager.
- Submit claims on the expense system **within 30 days** of the expense date. Claims submitted
  later than 30 days are not reimbursed unless Finance grants an exception.
- **Receipts are required for any expense over $25.** Expenses of $25 or less need a
  description but no receipt.

## 3. What is reimbursed

| Expense | Rule |
|---|---|
| Personal vehicle mileage | Current IRS standard mileage rate, for business trips only |
| Airfare | Economy class, booked at least 14 days ahead when possible |
| Hotel | Standard room; manager approval for more than 3 nights |
| Meals while traveling overnight | Up to $60 per day |
| Customer meals | Manager approval required in advance |

## 4. What is not reimbursed

- Commuting between home and your regular work location.
- Traffic or parking fines.
- Alcohol, unless pre-approved for a customer event.
- Home-office costs for remote work (see the Remote Work and Relocation Policy).

## 5. Approval and payment

1. Submit the claim with receipts in the expense system.
2. Your manager approves or rejects the claim.
3. Finance reviews approved claims and pays them with the next regular payroll.

## 6. Questions

Contact Finance through the expense system's help page.
