## 1. Purpose

This policy explains when office employees may work remotely and what to do before moving
to a new home address.

## 2. Who may work remotely

- **Operations roles** (warehouse associates, equipment operators, drivers) are on-site roles
  and are not eligible for remote work.
- **Office roles** may work remotely up to three days per week with their manager's agreement.
- Fully remote arrangements require the manager's approval and a written remote-work agreement.

## 3. Moving to a new address

If you plan to move, including to another state:

1. Tell your manager in writing at least **30 days** before the move.
2. Your **manager approves or declines** the move based on business needs.
3. After approval, update your home address on the HR portal before the move date.

## 4. Equipment and workspace

- Grayer provides a laptop for approved remote work. Other home-office costs are not
  reimbursed.
- Keep company equipment secure and lock your screen when away.

## 5. Questions

Contact your manager or People Operations.
