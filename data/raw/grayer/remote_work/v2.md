## 1. Purpose

This policy explains when office employees may work remotely and the approvals required before
moving to another state. It replaces version 1 for all moves with a move date on or after
2025-07-01.

## 2. What changed from version 1

- An out-of-state move now needs approval from **both your manager and People Operations (HR)**.
  Version 1 required manager approval only.
- The notice period for an out-of-state move is now **60 days**. Version 1 required 30 days.
- You may only relocate to a state where Grayer operates.

## 3. Who may work remotely

- **Operations roles** (warehouse associates, equipment operators, drivers) are on-site roles
  and are not eligible for remote work.
- **Office roles** may work remotely up to three days per week with their manager's agreement.
- Fully remote arrangements require a written remote-work agreement approved by the manager and
  People Operations.

## 4. Moving to another state

Grayer can employ remote and hybrid employees only in its operating states:
**Ohio, Indiana, Kentucky and Pennsylvania**. A move to any other state cannot be approved
while you remain in your current role.

| Step | Who | When |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Submit a relocation request on the HR portal | Employee | At least **60 days** before the move |
| 2. Review business need and role eligibility | Manager | Within 10 business days |
| 3. Review state, tax and payroll setup | People Operations (HR) | Within 10 business days of manager approval |
| 4. Confirm the decision in writing | People Operations (HR) | After both approvals |

You must have **both** approvals in writing before you move. Moving without approval may
result in a change of role or the end of the remote-work arrangement.

## 5. Moves within the same state

A move within your current state does not need approval. Update your address on the HR portal
within 10 days of moving.

## 6. Equipment and workspace

- Grayer provides a laptop for approved remote work. Other home-office costs are not
  reimbursed.
- Keep company equipment secure and lock your screen when away.

## 7. Questions

Contact your manager or People Operations. Managers can find approval steps in the Manager
Guide.
