## 1. Purpose

These guidelines tell People Operations how to set relocation assistance and pay adjustments
after a relocation has been approved under version 2 of the Remote Work and Relocation Policy.

## 2. Relocation assistance

| Type of move | Assistance |
|---|---|
| Company-requested move (Grayer asks the employee to move) | Lump sum of **$5,000**, paid with the first payroll after the move |
| Employee-requested move | **No relocation assistance** |
| Company-requested move with temporary housing need | Up to **30 days** of temporary housing, in addition to the lump sum |

If an employee leaves Grayer voluntarily within 12 months of receiving a lump sum, they must
repay it in full.

## 3. Pay adjustment by state

Base pay is adjusted by the location factor of the employee's new state. The adjustment takes
effect on the first full pay period after the move date.

| State | Location factor |
|---|---|
| Ohio (OH) | 1.00 |
| Indiana (IN) | 0.97 |
| Kentucky (KY) | 0.96 |
| Pennsylvania (PA) | 1.03 |

**Calculation:** new base pay = current base pay × (new state factor ÷ current state factor).
Example: an employee earning $60,000 who moves from Ohio to Kentucky has a new base pay of
$60,000 × (0.96 ÷ 1.00) = $57,600.

## 4. Payroll and tax setup

- Register state tax withholding for the new state before the first pay date after the move.
- Confirm the employee's work location and address in the HR system.
- Notify Benefits Administration in case plan availability differs by location.

## 5. Records

Store the approval records, assistance decision and pay calculation in the employee's HR file.
Do not share location factors with managers or employees; communicate only the employee's own
new base pay.
