## 1. Purpose

This guide tells managers how to handle relocation and PTO requests consistently. It
supplements, and does not change, the Remote Work and Relocation Policy and the PTO and Leave
Policy.

## 2. Relocation requests

Always use the Remote Work and Relocation Policy **version in effect on the requested move
date**. Check the HR portal for the current version; do not rely on memory of older rules.

1. **Check the role.** Operations roles are on-site and cannot relocate to work remotely.
2. **Check the destination and notice period** against that policy version. If the request
   does not meet them, decline it and suggest the employee contact People Operations.
3. **Decide within 10 business days.** Record your decision and reason on the HR portal.
4. **Check whether HR approval is also required** under that policy version. If it is, forward
   your approval to People Operations; the move is not approved until they confirm in writing.

Do not discuss pay changes with the employee. People Operations handles any pay adjustment
after a relocation is approved.

## 3. PTO requests

| Check | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Balance | The employee must have enough accrued PTO; do not approve negative balances |
| Notice | 3 days for 1–2 days off; 2 weeks for 3 or more consecutive days |
| Coverage | Operations sites: confirm minimum shift staffing before approving |
| Response time | Approve or decline within 3 business days |

- Approve requests fairly, generally in the order they were received.
- You may decline a request only for a documented business reason, such as minimum staffing.
- Sick-day PTO does not need advance approval.

## 4. Escalation

Contact People Operations if a request is unusual, if an employee disputes a decision, or if
a request may involve medical or family leave.
