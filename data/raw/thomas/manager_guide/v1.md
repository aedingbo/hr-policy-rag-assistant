## 1. Purpose

This guide helps managers handle employee moves and flexible PTO fairly.

## 2. Employee moves

- Moves within the operating states **do not need your approval**, and you may not block them.
  Your role is to plan for the new time zone.
- When an employee tells you about a move:
  1. Confirm they have notified People Operations, as the Remote Work and Relocation Policy
     requires.
  2. Agree how they will keep overlapping with the team's core hours.
  3. Update on-call rotations and meeting times if needed.
- If the destination is outside the operating states, direct the employee to People Operations
  before they commit to the move.

## 3. Approving flexible PTO

| Check | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Coverage | Make sure releases, on-call and customer commitments are covered |
| Fairness | Approve similar requests in a similar way across the team |
| Notice | Use the notice periods in the PTO and Leave Policy as guidance |
| Response time | Respond within 2 business days |

- Decline only for a specific business reason, and offer alternative dates.
- Sick days do not need your approval.

## 4. Making sure people rest

Flexible PTO only works if people actually use it. Twice a year, review each team member's time
off. If someone has taken fewer than 10 days in the past 12 months, talk with them about
planning time off.

## 5. Escalation

Contact People Operations about disputes, repeated coverage problems, or anything that may
involve medical, family or parental leave.
