## 1. Purpose

Thomas Software is remote-first. This policy explains where you may work from and what to do
before you move.

## 2. Where you may work

- Every role may be performed fully remotely.
- You may live and work in any of Thomas Software's operating states: **Colorado, Texas,
  Washington, New York, Illinois and Arizona**.
- Working temporarily from another location (for example while traveling) is allowed for up to
  30 days per year within the United States.

## 3. Moving within the operating states

**No approval is needed** to move between or within the operating states. You must:

1. **Notify People Operations (HR) at least 30 days before your move**, using the relocation
   form on the HR portal.
2. Tell your manager so the team calendar shows your new time zone.
3. Update your home address on the HR portal by your move date.

People Operations uses the notice to set up payroll and tax registration for your new state.

## 4. Moving outside the operating states

Thomas Software cannot employ people in states where it does not operate. If you plan to move
outside the six operating states, contact People Operations **before** committing to the move.

## 5. Equipment

Your laptop and monitor are shipped to your home. Home-office costs are covered by the
home-office stipend (see the Expense and Reimbursement Policy).

## 6. Questions

Contact People Operations through the HR portal.
