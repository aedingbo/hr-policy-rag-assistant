## 1. Purpose

This policy explains flexible PTO and other leave at Thomas Software.

## 2. Flexible PTO

- Thomas Software has **flexible PTO**: there is no fixed annual allowance and PTO does not
  accrue.
- Take the time you need, **with your manager's approval**, while keeping your work and team
  commitments covered.
- We encourage everyone to take **at least 10 days of PTO each year**. Managers review time off
  with their teams twice a year to make sure people are resting.
- Because PTO does not accrue, there is no carryover and no payout of unused PTO.

## 3. Requesting PTO

| Length | Notice |
|---|---|
| 1–2 days | As early as possible; at least 2 business days |
| 3–9 days | At least 2 weeks |
| 10 or more consecutive days | At least 1 month |

Submit requests in the HR portal and add the dates to the team calendar.

## 4. Sick time

Sick days are not counted against flexible PTO and do not need advance approval. Tell your
manager as soon as you can.

## 5. Company holidays

Ten company holidays per year, plus a company-wide week off between December 24 and January 1.
The holiday calendar is published on the HR portal each November.

## 6. Other leave

| Leave type | Length | Paid? |
|---|---|---|
| Parental leave | 16 weeks | Yes, full pay |
| Bereavement | Up to 5 days | Yes |
| Medical or family leave | Up to 12 weeks for eligible employees | Paid at 60% after the first week |

Contact People Operations to request parental, bereavement, medical or family leave.
