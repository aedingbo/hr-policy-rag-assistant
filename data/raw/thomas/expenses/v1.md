## 1. Purpose

This policy explains business expense reimbursement and the home-office stipend.

## 2. General rules

- Expenses must be reasonable and necessary for Thomas Software business.
- Submit claims **within 90 days** of the expense date. Later claims are not reimbursed.
- **Receipts are required for any expense over $75.**

## 3. Home-office stipend

| Stipend | Amount | How |
|---|---|---|
| Home-office setup (one time) | Up to **$600** | Claim with receipts within your first 90 days |
| Internet and phone (monthly) | **$60 per month** | Paid automatically with payroll; no receipts needed |

The setup stipend covers items such as a desk, chair, headset or monitor arm. Items bought with
the stipend belong to you.

## 4. Travel

| Expense | Rule |
|---|---|
| Team gatherings and customer visits | Manager approval in advance |
| Airfare | Economy class |
| Hotel | Standard room |
| Meals while traveling | Up to $75 per day |

## 5. What is not reimbursed

- Coworking space memberships, unless approved by your manager.
- Fines, personal entertainment and alcohol outside approved team events.

## 6. How to submit

1. Submit the claim in the expense system with receipts where required.
2. Your manager approves it.
3. Finance pays approved claims with the next payroll.
