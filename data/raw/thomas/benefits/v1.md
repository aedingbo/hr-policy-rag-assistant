## 1. Purpose

This document explains the benefits Thomas Software offers, who is eligible and how to enroll.

## 2. Eligibility

- All full-time employees (30 or more hours per week) are eligible for medical, dental and vision
  coverage **from their first day of employment**. There is no waiting period.
- Part-time employees are eligible for the retirement plan and the employee assistance program.

## 3. Plans offered

| Benefit | Options | Employee cost |
|---|---|---|
| Medical | PPO or HDHP with HSA | Company pays 90% of employee premium |
| Dental | One plan | Company-paid for employees |
| Vision | One plan | Company-paid for employees |
| Life insurance | 2× annual salary | Company-paid |
| Employee assistance program | Free confidential counseling | Company-paid |

## 4. Retirement plan

Employees may join the 401(k) plan from day one. Thomas matches 100% of contributions up to 5% of
eligible pay, vesting immediately.

## 5. How to enroll

1. You receive an enrollment link on your first day.
2. Make your elections on the HR portal **within 30 days** of your start date. Coverage is
   backdated to your first day.
3. If you miss the window, you can enroll at open enrollment (each November) or after a
   qualifying life event.

## 6. Questions

Contact Benefits Administration through the HR portal.
