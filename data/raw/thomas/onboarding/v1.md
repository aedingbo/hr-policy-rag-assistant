## 1. Purpose

This guide explains how your first weeks at Thomas Software work — fully remote.

## 2. Before your first day

- **Your laptop is shipped before day one** so it arrives at least two business days before you
  start. If it has not arrived three days before your start date, contact IT.
- Complete the new-hire forms and identity verification sent by Talent Acquisition.

## 3. Your buddy

Every new hire is paired with a **buddy for the first two weeks**: an experienced colleague
outside your direct team who answers questions, explains how things work and meets with you at
least three times a week.

## 4. First two weeks

| Day / week | Activity |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Video welcome session; laptop setup with IT; accounts and multi-factor authentication |
| Days 2–5 | Security training (required in week one); team introductions; read your team's guides |
| Week 2 | First small task with your team; daily check-in with your buddy |

## 5. First 90 days

Your manager sets goals with you in week two and reviews them at 30, 60 and 90 days. Benefits
start on day one (see the Benefits Overview and Enrollment).

## 6. Questions

Contact Talent Acquisition or your buddy.
